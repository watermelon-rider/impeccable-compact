# Expressive craft floor

Load immediately before a UI edit. The selected style controls expression; this
floor protects the product underneath it.

## One dominant move

- Commit to one spatial, material, or typographic idea.
- Support it with quieter structure. Do not collect unrelated effects.
- Use motion for hierarchy, state, progress, or mechanism, never continuous noise.
- Keep a stable static state and a reduced-motion equivalent.

## Preserve use

- Navigation, controls, and form behavior remain recognizable.
- Body and control contrast meet accessible thresholds.
- Keyboard order, visible focus, zoom, text overflow, empty, loading, error, and
  disabled states remain complete.
- Expensive filters, blur, canvas, or WebGL need a lightweight fallback.

## Refuse empty spectacle

- No gradient text, decorative glass, generic glow fields, or floating geometry
  without a product meaning.
- No raw texture that makes copy unreadable or controls uncertain.
- No effect that hides the primary action, proof, or recovery route.
- No invented claims, metrics, customers, or assets.

## Verify once

Inspect the real desktop and mobile render once after the edit batch. Check
first-viewport hierarchy, legibility, overflow, focus, and whether the expressive
mechanism is understandable without an explanation.
